enterprise_d.glb in this folder is a modified copy (tools/prepare-enterprise.mjs): scaled to the ship's real 642.5 m with the bow on +x and the
lowest hull point on y = 0, the meshes welded and decimated (1 199 440 to 239 884 triangles), merged into one mesh per section (saucer, drive,
nacelles, aux), the tangents dropped, the metallic factor lowered to 0.25 (the game has no environment map), the materials marked
extras.glow so the game can boost the emissive maps, the sixteen 1024 px textures converted from PNG to WebP, and the whole compressed
(meshopt). The textures' content is unchanged. The game credits it in the F1 help, README.md and CHANGELOG.md.
