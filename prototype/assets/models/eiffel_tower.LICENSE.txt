eiffel_tower.glb in this folder is a modified copy: simplified/quantized and meshopt-compressed with
gltf-transform (EXT_meshopt_compression + KHR_mesh_quantization) for the desktop build; the materials are
replaced in-game with a plain brown PBR material (#6b4a32, roughness 0.75, metalness 0.35). The game credits
the model in the F1 help, README.md and CHANGELOG.md.
