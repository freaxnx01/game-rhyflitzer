# Reference photos

Own photos taken on 2026-09-29 around Sisseln and Sisslerfeld. They are **references** for modelling and for checking the generated world, not game textures (except where noted). Metadata (EXIF: camera, time) stripped; scaled to 2000 px. Frames without readable number plates or people were picked.

| File | Shows | Used for |
|---|---|---|
| `2026-09-29_sisseln-hauptstrasse-cycle-lanes.jpg` | Hauptstrasse Sisseln with yellow broken cycle-lane lines, no centre line | Road markings; source of `prototype/textures/asphalt_sisseln.jpg` (rectified, tiled) |
| `2026-09-29_sisseln-climb-1-left-bend.jpg` | Climb from the Sissle toward the village (direction Laufenburg): left bend, solid white centre line | Checking the climb and bend from OSM + swissALTI3D |
| `2026-09-29_sisseln-climb-2-retaining-wall.jpg` | Same climb: block-stone retaining wall on the left | Terrain cut, embankments |
| `2026-09-29_sisseln-climb-3-top-into-village.jpg` | Top of the climb, straight into the village, school warning sign | Checking the terrace edge |
| `2026-09-29_plattform-sisslerfeld.jpg` | Plattform Sisslerfeld (wooden viewing tower) | Hero asset model. The sponsor banner is **not** modelled (docs/07) |
| `2026-09-29_plattform-and-dsm-chimney.jpg` | Plattform Sisslerfeld, DSM-Firmenich chimney (red/white bands) and plant buildings | Chimney bands, industrial palette |

The three climb frames come from a short dashcam-style video (8 s); the video itself is not in the repo.
