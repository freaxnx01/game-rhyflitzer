# Background: Midtown Madness and European cities

Research notes from the kickoff chat (2026-09-28).

## The original games

| Game | Year | Platform | Cities |
|---|---|---|---|
| Midtown Madness | 1999 | PC | Chicago |
| Midtown Madness 2 | 2000 | PC | San Francisco, London |
| Midtown Madness 3 | 2003 | Xbox | Paris, Washington D.C. |

- MM1 and MM2 were developed by Angel Studios (now Rockstar San Diego), published by Microsoft.
- MM3 is playable today via xemu or original hardware; online play is back through the Insignia replacement servers.

## Fan-made European content for MM2

Real European towns are practically absent from the MM2 modding scene. What exists are fictional European-style maps:

- **Eurocity** (Sajmon14, 2012): European-style landscape with a small town, traffic, animated train, hudmap, several race modes. Known to crash with MM2Hook installed.
- **Neustadt V2**: fictional German village (soccer field, farm, corn field, roundabout).
- **Tolosa Testing Center** (Pibraclab, Franch88): track/circuit map, not a town.
- **Megamod 2SE**: large overhaul of the stock San Francisco and London.

Well-known real-city ports are American (e.g. New York, ported from Midnight Club).

## Community

- mm2x.com (Midtown Madness 2 eXtreme): largest MM2 modding archive, incl. city tools
- ModDB (Midtown Madness 2): active releases, many require NuHook (Lua hook)
- Midtown Club Discord: where the current scene posts releases
- Racing Madness Wiki (Fandom)

## Takeaway

There is no way to race Hochrhein towns in any Midtown Madness game. That gap is the reason for this project.
