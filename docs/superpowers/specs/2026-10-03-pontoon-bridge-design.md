# Pontoon bridge: build and remove a crossing where there is no bridge (#100)

Status: approved in headless `/enrich` (quick mode, no human gate) 2026-10-03 · Issue #100

## Goal

Issue #100: „Wo keine Brücke ist, soll eine Pontonierbrücke/Behelfsbrücke gebaut und wieder abgebaut werden können." Related: #76 (bridges and underpasses).

Success: drive to the bank of the Rhine where no bridge is. The HUD shows „X · Pontonbrücke bauen". Press **X**: within three seconds a military-style pontoon bridge is laid across the river, bay by bay, from your bank to the opposite bank, straight across the water and onto the nearest road over there. You drive over it like over any bridge. Press **X** again: the bridge is taken back, bay by bay, from the far end. A car still on it drops into the Rhine („Sleep with the fishes!") and comes back on the last road, as it does today. There is at most one pontoon bridge at a time.

**User decision (2026-10-03):** at the Rhine bank the game shows a prompt „Pontonbrücke bauen"; a key builds the bridge, animated in a few seconds, across to the opposite bank; the same key removes it again; at most one bridge at a time.

## Starting point (verified 2026-10-03 on `main` @ `3f62059`)

- **Water.** `riverDist(x, z)` (`prototype/index.html:383`) is the water signed distance field from the world file (`sdfSampler`, `prototype/world.js:22`; 8 m grid, Int8 clamped to ±120, 127 outside the grid; negative inside water). `WATER = waterIndex(L.water)` (`index.html:400`, `world.js:36`) answers `levelAt` and `nameAt` by polygon. The Rhine is 45 polygons named `Rhein`, chunked every ~500 m, with levels −2.8 … −1.4 below the Säckingen weir (x < −700) and 5.4 … 5.7 above it. The Sissle is **not** a water polygon: it is a centre-line `stream` (`w` 12), drawn by `streamH`/`STREAM_GRID` (`index.html:446`), and `WATER.nameAt` returns `''` there. Ponds and the weir basin are polygons without the name `Rhein`. In the hand-traced layout the river is `RIVER` (`index.html:325`), `riverDist` is the segment distance minus `RIVER.hw`, and `WATER` is `null`.
- **Bridge mechanics.** `onBridge(x, z, y)` (`index.html:377`) queries `BRIDGE_GRID` for `OSM_BRIDGES` pieces `{ r: { pts, w }, len, hw, kind, h0, h1 }`, tests `nearestOnPolyline` within `hw` and `bridgeAccepts(bridgeSurfaceAt(b, t), y)` (`world.js:96-102`). `groundH` (`index.html:449`) asks `onBridge` first and returns the deck surface; otherwise water → river bed (`wl − 3`); `waterLevelAt` (`:437`) returns `null` on a bridge. `BRIDGE_GRID` and `OBB_GRID` are append-only spatial grids filled at start-up (`gridAdd`, `world.js:6`); there is no removal.
- **Car in water.** `stepCar` (`index.html:1083`): `wl !== null` → splash, drag, sink to `wl − 1.2`, toast `fishes` after 0.35 s, `resetCar()` after 2.8 s. `P.safe` is only updated on a road and never on a bridge (`:1084`), and `resetCar` (`:1018`) places the car at `P.safe` with `groundH(x, z, -Infinity)`.
- **Runtime objects.** The helicopter (`index.html:954`) is a `THREE.Group` with its own `MeshLambertMaterial`s added to the scene, outside the merged static `MESH` roles. That is the pattern for a thing that appears and disappears during play.
- **Keys** (`index.html:973`): taken are B, C, E, F, G, H, I (reserved), J, K, L (#2), M, N, O (#18), P/Esc, Q, R, T, V, Tab, Enter, Space, F1, F3, and the driving keys W A S D, arrows, Ctrl, Shift. Free: U, X, Y, Z. `grep -c KeyX prototype/index.html` → 0.
- **Loop** (`index.html:1245`): while `PAUSE.on` only the toast fade, the debug tick and the render run; otherwise `stepCar`/`stepFly`, `stepRace`, `stepCamera`, `hud`, sound and render. `__mm.sim(x, z, th, v, secs, hold)` steps `stepCar` at 60 Hz (`:1061`).
- **Race flags.** A jump sets `R.jumped`, a flight `R.flown`, the Holzbrücke `R.shortcut` (`stepRace`, `:1122`); `resultHtml` (`:1124`) appends `notCountedJump` / `notCountedHeli` / `viaHolz`. `__mm.raceFlags()` (`:1043`) reports `jumped` and `flown`.
- **HUD** (`index.html:109-111`): `#watername`, `#roadname` lines, refreshed every 250 ms in `hud()` (`:1240`), and `#toast`. Strings via `tr()` from `prototype/strings.js` (en/de, same keys, Swiss spelling, `strings.test.mjs` enforces key parity). The F1 help lists one `<kbd>` line per key (`:119-140`).
- **Minimap.** `drawMap` (`:1208`) draws the static map then checkpoints, finish and car through `mapPt`.
- **Tests.** `node --test prototype/tests/*.test.mjs` for pure modules; pytest + Playwright in `prototype/tests/test_*.py`, `needs_world` skip for the tracked `data/world_hochrhein.json`, terrain blocked by default.
- **Dry run on the world file (2026-10-03).** The Sisseln bank between the Sissle mouth and Murg has one road point within 30 m of the Rhine: Innermattstrasse (1404.4, −425.5), 20.3 m from the water. The SDF gradient there points (−0.02, −1.00); the water edge is 17 m north, the Rhine (level 5.53) is 208 m wide there, the far bank lies at 225 m and the far-side road (Murger Weg, `service`) 74 m beyond the far water edge. No bridge is within kilometres.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Key | **X** builds and removes (toggle). Mnemonic „cross the Rhine"; `KeyX` is the same physical key on US, Swiss and German layouts (Y/Z are swapped there). Only with `$('overlay').hidden`, not while `FLY.on`, not while paused. |
| Prompt | A new HUD line `#prompt` under `#roadname`: `tr('pontoonBuild')` („X · build a pontoon bridge" / „X · Pontonbrücke bauen") while no bridge exists and the car is at a Rhine bank; `tr('pontoonRemove')` („X · remove the pontoon bridge" / „X · Pontonbrücke abbauen") while a bridge exists, wherever the car is; empty otherwise. Refreshed in the 250 ms HUD block. |
| At the bank | The car is on land and `riverDist(x, z)` is in `[0, reach]`, `reach = 30` m. The bank normal is minus the SDF gradient (central differences, ±4 m); a flat gradient (< 0.2) is „not at a bank". Marching along the normal in 1 m steps reaches water within `reach`, and `nameAt` 2 m inside is `'Rhein'`. Everything else — Sissle, ponds, weir basin, mid-water, on a bridge — is not a bank: toast `pontoonNoBank` („No Rhine bank here" / „Hier ist kein Rheinufer"). Hand layout: `nameAt` is `() => 'Rhein'`. |
| Crossing line | Perpendicular to the river: along the bank normal from the car's position. The far bank is where `riverDist ≥ 0` again, refined by bisection to 0.1 m; no far bank within `maxWidth = 400` m → toast `pontoonNoFarBank` („No opposite bank within reach" / „Kein gegenüberliegendes Ufer in Reichweite"). The far end continues onto land to the first point on a road (`roadDist ≤ 0`) within `roadReach = 80` m, else `apron = 6` m past the water edge. The line is swept at 0°, ±5°, …, ±25°; the pick is the candidate that reaches a road, shortest first, else the shortest that reaches land. The near end is `apron` = 6 m inland from the near water edge along the line. |
| Where there is no bridge | Points every 10 m along the line are tested with `bridgeNear(x, z)`: `gridQuery(BRIDGE_GRID, x, z, 25)` with any piece whose `kind !== 'rail'` (hand layout: `BRIDGES` segment distance ≤ 25). A hit → toast `pontoonHasBridge` („There is a bridge here already" / „Hier gibt es schon eine Brücke"). |
| One bridge | `PONTOON.deck` is `null` or one deck. X with a deck present toggles its direction (`dir` −1 = removing, +1 = building), whatever the car's position. |
| Deck geometry | `deck = { a, b, len, ux, uz, hw: 2.2, deckH, hNear, hFar, rampNear, rampFar, nBays, built, dir }`. `deckH` = water level at the midpoint (`WATER.levelAt`, else 0) + `deckAbove` 0.9 m (0.5 m freeboard + 0.4 m deck). `hNear`/`hFar` = `groundH(a, 1e4)` / `groundH(b, 1e4)`. Ramps: from each land end the surface is linear to `deckH`, reached at `ramp = clamp(|h − deckH| / maxGrade, 6, 60)` m with `maxGrade = 0.15`; if both ramps overlap they are scaled to meet. `pontoonSurfaceAt(deck, t)` is that piecewise-linear profile; no 0.3 m `bridgeDeckOffset` and no end plateau: a point past either end is not on the deck. |
| Drivable | `onBridge` tests the pontoon first: `pontoonHit(PONTOON.deck, x, z, y)` → `{ b: deck, t, pontoon: true }` when `0 ≤ t ≤ drivableLength(deck)`, `|s| ≤ hw` and `bridgeAccepts(surface, y)`. `groundH` returns `pontoonSurfaceAt` for `ob.pontoon`. `drivableLength` = whole built bays × `bay` 6 m, so the car only drives on what is drawn. The deck never enters `OSM_BRIDGES`, `BRIDGE_GRID` or `OBB_GRID`. |
| Build and removal | `stepPontoon(dt)`: `built += dir × dt / buildSecs`, `buildSecs = 3`, clamped to [0, 1]; `built` reaching 0 while removing deletes the deck and its meshes. Building runs from the near bank; removal retracts from the far end (last laid, first taken). Stepped inside the loop's step block, so it freezes while paused (#83). A car beyond the drivable front has no deck under it: `waterLevelAt` returns the river → the existing splash → reset to `P.safe`, the last road before the bridge. No new code for that. |
| Look | Low-poly military pontoon bridge, built in code per bay (6 m): a deck slab 4.4 × 0.3 m with its top at the surface (olive-grey planks `#6e6a50`), rail posts 0.9 m at ±(hw + 0.1) every bay end with a rail bar (`#9a9d86`), and under every floating bay (surface at `deckH`) one pontoon hull: a box 8.0 m across the deck × 1.8 m along × 1.1 m high, olive drab `#4b5a3a`, top at `deckH − 0.4`, so 0.5 m shows above the water. Ramp bays stand on two trestle legs 0.3 × 0.3 m from the slab down to the terrain instead of a hull. Three shared `MeshLambertMaterial`s in a `THREE.Group pontoon` on the scene; one child group per bay; geometry disposed on removal. A newly laid bay drops in: it appears 1.5 m high and settles over 0.4 s (`bayDrop`). The implementation ends with a **screenshot step for a human** (Playwright screenshot from the near bank, committed under `design/screenshots/`) because the look cannot be unit-tested. |
| Minimap | While a deck exists, `drawMap` draws the line a→b in olive (`#8a8f5a`, 3 px with a dark outline), under the checkpoints. The Tab full map is the same canvas. |
| Race | Driving on the pontoon deck while `armed`/`racing` sets `R.pontoon = true` (in `stepRace`, next to the Holzbrücke check). `finish` records no best time (like `jumped`/`flown`) and `resultHtml` appends `notCountedPontoon` („with a pontoon bridge, not counted · " / „mit Pontonbrücke, zählt nicht · "). `startRace` clears it; `__mm.raceFlags()` gains `pontoon`. |
| Strings | New keys en/de: `pontoonBuild`, `pontoonRemove`, `pontoonBuilding` („Pontoniers at work!" / „Pontoniere ans Werk!"), `pontoonRemoving` („Pontoon bridge coming down" / „Pontonbrücke wird abgebaut"), `pontoonNoBank`, `pontoonNoFarBank`, `pontoonHasBridge`, `notCountedPontoon`, `keyPontoon` („pontoon bridge: build · remove (at the Rhine bank)" / „Pontonbrücke: bauen · abbauen (am Rheinufer)"). F1 help line `<kbd>X</kbd>` after the F line. |
| Pure logic | New module `prototype/pontoon.js` (no DOM, no three.js): `PONTOON_CFG`, `bankNormal`, `marchToWater`, `marchToLand`, `crossingLine(env, x, z)`, `pontoonSurfaceAt`, `pontoonLocal`, `drivableLength`, `pontoonHit`, `stepPontoon`, `togglePontoon`, `bayFrames`, `bayDrop`, `pontoonHulls`, `pontoonPrompt`. `env = { dist, nameAt, waterLevel, roadAt, bridgeNear, ground, cfg }` so tests use a synthetic SDF. `index.html` keeps the glue (`PONTOON` state, meshes, key, HUD, race flag). |
| Test hooks | `__mm.pontoon()` → `{ on, built, dir, len, a, b, deckH, nBays, prompt, error }` (`error` = the last refused reason), `__mm.pontoonSim(secs)` steps `stepPontoon` at 60 Hz, `__mm.pressPontoon()` runs the X handler. |
| Hand layout | Works the same: `nameAt` is constant `'Rhein'`, `waterLevel` is 0, `bridgeNear` uses the hand `BRIDGES`. |

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `prototype/pontoon.js` is pure and unit-tested; on a synthetic straight river `crossingLine` returns a line perpendicular to the bank from 6 m inland on the near side to the first road point on the far side, refuses „not at a bank" (too far, in the water, not the Rhine), „no far bank" (wider than 400 m) and „bridge nearby", and the sweep finds a far-side road the perpendicular misses.
- [ ] `pontoonSurfaceAt` is `deckH` over the water and meets `hNear`/`hFar` exactly at the ends with a grade ≤ 15 % where the ramps fit; `pontoonHit` accepts only points within the width and the built length, and respects the 1.5 m `bridgeAccepts` rule.
- [ ] `stepPontoon` completes a build in 3 s, `togglePontoon` reverses it, and a removal ends with the deck gone; `drivableLength` grows in whole 6 m bays.
- [ ] OSM world (terrain blocked), car at Innermattstrasse (1404.4, −425.5) heading north: the HUD prompt reads „X · Pontonbrücke bauen" / „X · build a pontoon bridge"; **X** starts a build (`__mm.pontoon().on`, `len` 290 ± 30 m, `b` on Murger Weg); after `pontoonSim(3)` `built` is 1 and `groundH` at the midpoint equals `deckH` (the game's water level + 0.9; with the terrain blocked the level is 0, `index.html:437`, so `deckH` = 0.9); `__mm.sim` from `a` along the line at 12 m/s for 35 s crosses with `bridge: true` and no water, and ends on the far bank on land.
- [ ] **X** again: `dir` is −1; after `pontoonSim(3)` `__mm.pontoon().on` is false, `groundH` at the midpoint is the river bed again, and a car placed mid-river during the removal ends in the water (`__mm.car().water !== null`).
- [ ] **X** 200 m from the river toasts „Hier ist kein Rheinufer" / „No Rhine bank here"; on the Fridolinsbrücke approach it toasts „Hier gibt es schon eine Brücke" / „There is a bridge here already"; nothing is built in either case.
- [ ] Driving on the pontoon deck during a race sets `raceFlags().pontoon`, and the result says „mit Pontonbrücke, zählt nicht".
- [ ] While paused, `built` does not change. The existing node and Playwright suites stay green (`sinkCheck`, `grassOverRoad`, the Holzbrücke rail test, the overpass test).
- [ ] A screenshot of the finished bridge from the near bank is committed for the human review of the look.

## Interactions and landing order

- **#78 (Fridolinsbrücke deck) and #76 (rail bridges):** both edit `OSM_BRIDGES`, `fillBridgeHeights`, `bridgeDeckOffset` flags and the hero grouping. The pontoon touches none of those: it adds one guard line at the top of `onBridge` and one branch in `groundH`, and reads `BRIDGE_GRID` only through `bridgeNear`. #78's `pastEnd`/approach change and this guard sit in the same `onBridge` function, so **whichever lands second rebases a one-line conflict**. Recommended order: **#78 → #76 → #100**, since the first two are already planned and the pontoon is independent of them. #76's `kind: 'rail'` pieces are excluded from `bridgeNear` so a rail deck never blocks a pontoon (no rail bridge crosses the Rhine in the clip anyway).
- **#101 (submarine, enriched in parallel):** pontoons are obstacles. `pontoonHulls(deck)` returns one OBB-style box per laid floating bay `{ x, z, hw: 0.9, hd: 4.0, c, s, y0: deckH − 1.5, y1: deckH − 0.4 }` (`hw` along the deck, `hd` across it, `c`/`s` the deck direction, absolute heights); the deck underside is `deckH − 0.4` over `drivableLength(deck)`. #101 reads `PONTOON.deck` through `pontoonHulls` each step; this issue provides the function and nothing in `collide` or `OBB_GRID`. The `bridgeAccepts` rule (ground only from 1.5 m below the surface) already keeps a submerged vehicle off the deck.
- **#18 (autopilot):** its road graph is built from `ROADS` once; the pontoon deck is not a road, so the autopilot **never routes over it** and ignores it. If a route is wanted later, #18 adds a temporary edge a→b while a deck exists. Nothing here.
- **#83 (pause, merged):** `stepPontoon` runs in the unpaused step block, so the build freezes; X is swallowed by the pause menu like every other game key.
- **#105 (damage):** water never hurts, so a car dropped by a removal takes no damage; the deck is ground like any bridge, so a hard landing on it hurts as a landing does. No shared code.
- **#10 (helicopter, merged):** X does nothing while `FLY.on`; landing on the pontoon deck is not special (`landingSpot` looks for roads).

## Out of scope

- Rails that block the car (no runtime `OBB_GRID` entries): a car steered off the 4.4 m deck falls into the Rhine. Sound for the build. Pontoon bridges over the Sissle or other water. Several bridges at once. Saving the bridge across reloads. Autopilot routing over it (#18). Submarine collision code (#101).

## Assumptions

- **A1** [high] [confirmed] At the Rhine bank a prompt „Pontonbrücke bauen" shows; one key builds the bridge across to the opposite bank, animated in a few seconds; the same key removes it; at most one bridge at a time. Decided by the user on 2026-10-03.
- **A2** [med] The key is **X**. Rejected: U (no mnemonic), Y/Z (swapped between Swiss/German and US layouts; the game reads `e.code`), Enter/Space as a contextual key (horn and gas, `index.html:973`, `:1079`). Free letters per `index.html:973` and the #18/#2/#83 specs: U, X, Y, Z.
- **A3** [med] „At the bank" = on land within 30 m of the water, the crossing is perpendicular (minus the SDF gradient), the far end runs to the first road within 80 m with a ±25° sweep. Rejected: starting at the nearest road end (banks here rarely have a road end at the water, dry run: one road point within 30 m on the whole Sisseln bank); a graph search for the far road (over-engineering for a 240 m line). Evidence: `prototype/world.js:22-33` (SDF), dry run above (far road at 74 m).
- **A4** [high] Rhine only, by `WATER.nameAt === 'Rhein'` 2 m inside the water edge. The Sissle is a stream, not a polygon (`index.html:446`, `streamNameAt`), so it never qualifies. User requirement (not the Sissle).
- **A5** [med] The deck floats 0.9 m above the water with ramps of at most 15 % at the banks (6–60 m long). Rejected: linear `h0 → h1` like `bridgeDeckAt` (a pontoon deck is flat on the water; linear would sag or climb mid-river where the banks differ, cf. #78's V).
- **A6** [med] Removal retracts from the far end over 3 s, and a car left without a deck falls into the Rhine and resets as today (`stepCar`, `index.html:1083`). Rejected: teleporting the car to a bank (new code for a rare case; the splash is the Midtown Madness homage the game already has); refusing to remove while a car is on it (the user asked for a plain toggle).
- **A7** [med] A race driven over a pontoon bridge is not counted, like a jump or a flight. Rejected: counted with a note like the Holzbrücke (`viaHolz`) — the Holzbrücke is a real bridge anyone can take, the pontoon is a tool the player conjures, so it belongs with J and F (`resultHtml`, `index.html:1124`).
- **A8** [med] The pontoon is a separate runtime object checked before `BRIDGE_GRID` in `onBridge`, not an entry in `OSM_BRIDGES`. Rejected: adding and removing grid entries (`gridAdd`, `world.js:6`, is append-only, and #78/#76 both rework that block).
- **A9** [med] „Where there is no bridge" = no non-rail bridge piece within 25 m of any point of the line. Rejected: a wider corridor (would block the whole Bad Säckingen stretch between Holzbrücke and Fridolinsbrücke, 350 m apart).
- **A10** [med] No collision rails on the deck. Rejected: runtime wall OBBs (`OBB_GRID` has no removal; `collide` at `index.html:1071` reads the grid only). The Holzbrücke rails exist because its walls are static.
- **A11** [med] The look (olive deck, drab hulls, trestles on the ramps, drop-in animation) is a code-built low-poly model with a committed screenshot for human review. Rejected: a textured model asset (the game has none; every object is code).
- **A12** [med] The minimap shows the deck as an olive line. Rejected: nothing on the map (the player would not find a bridge built 2 km away).

## Consequences

- With a bridge every 3 s on demand, the Rhine stops being a barrier for exploration; races keep their meaning because a pontoon run is not counted.
- `onBridge` gains one cheap test per call (a local-frame transform) when a deck exists, and none otherwise.
- The prompt line adds a fourth text line to the HUD stack (water, road, prompt); on phones it is one more line above the touch buttons.
- While a deck exists `P.safe` is never updated on it, so a reset after a splash returns the car to the last road before the bridge, possibly on the other bank.
- Trees, props and placement code are static and ignore the deck; a pontoon end may land on a tree or a lamp on the far bank.
