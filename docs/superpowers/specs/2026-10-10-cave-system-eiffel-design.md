# Reservoir Hübel: a cave system with a life-size Eiffel Tower (#201) — design

Status: enriched 2026-10-10 (no human gate; `[needs maintainer]` items are open) · Issue #201 · supersedes the geometry of `2026-10-09-hideout-eiffel-design.md` (#102), keeps its entrance, discovery and J entry.

## Goal

Playtest feedback on the #102 hideout: the Eiffel Tower is visible from the gate through the tunnel, the hall is small, the 33 m tower looks puny and is the wrong colour, and there is no room for more. Wanted: a **cave system** of several rooms behind the Reservoir Hübel gate, a **much bigger hall** that need not fit the hill, **life-size** objects (Eiffel Tower now, Atomium / Titanic later), the tower **not visible from the entrance**, the outside world **unchanged** (nothing shows from above). Desktop is the performance target.

Success: drive through the gate as today, down the 105 m tunnel into the Vorhalle (the old cavern, now a hub with lamps and two bricked-up side passages), turn right into a 90 m passage, round a bend — and the end of a 170 m tunnel is filled by the floodlit legs of a **330 m Eiffel Tower** standing in a dark hall 340 m across whose ceiling is lost in the gloom. Drive between its legs. From the Hübel, the hilltop, the helicopter and the map nothing has changed. **J** → `reservoir` still lands you at the gate; the "Hideout found" toast now fires in the hall.

## Starting point (verified 2026-10-10 on `main` @ `4e1ea73`)

- **The #102 hideout** is two cuts (`HIDE.tunnel`, `HIDE.cavern`, `prototype/hideout.js:32-40`) riding the rail-underpass patch machinery (`patchCuts`, `cutFloorMin`, `buildCuts`, `index.html:636-660`), a **lid** of the uncut hill (`meshH`) drawn over the roofed part into `grass` plus a reversed copy 0.6 m lower into `stone` as the ceiling (`:1075-1098`), trough walls + a ring wall (`hideoutBuild`, `:885-919`), the gate with the „RESERVOIR HÜBEL" sign, a gravel floor, two `PointLight(0xffd9a0, 120, 36, 2)` and four lamp posts, and the 1:10 primitive tower on the `dome` role with four foot OBBs (`eiffelTower`, `:922-935`). Every OBB is `secret: true` (the static map skips it).
- **Where the cave is known to the rest of the game:** `roofedAt` / `underLid` / `lidH` / `skyGroundH` (`:663-667`); `groundH` treats anything above the lid as standing on the hill (`:690`); `takeOff` refuses under a roof (`:1748`); `stepFly` uses `skyGroundH` (`:1751`); the find is `inCavern(P) && P.y < lidH` (`:1901`); the chase camera is held under the ceiling by `underCeiling` (`:1924`, `:1931`); trees skip `inHideout` (`:1304`, `:1317`) and forest-edge walls skip `segmentInHideout` (`:1318`); hooks `__mm.hideout/roofed/lidAt/skyGround/topAt/hideoutLights/hideoutRing` (`:1803-1808`).
- **The static map paints `terrainH`** (`:2099`): over a cut it paints the floor height, so a 340 m lowered disc would show as a lighter patch on the Tab map.
- **Forest trees** (`window.__FOREST`, OSM woods, `:1419`) are `InstancedMesh`es per 512 m tile (`FOREST_MESHES`); their height is `terrainH` at placement. The scattered random `TREES` are one region-wide `InstancedMesh`. One OSM forest polygon covers the whole hill south of the Hübel.
- **Lighting/look switching** has a precedent: `setUnderwater(on)` (`:1639`) swaps `scene.fog`, the sky uniforms, `hemi`/`sun` intensity and hides the sun sprite; `applyStyle` (`:1635`) restores per style. Lights in three r170 are physical (cd); the sun is `0.8` (`:1360`), shadows only in the smooth style (`STYLES`, `:1629-1632`), shadow camera 360 m around the car.
- **Terrain** (`data/terrain_hochrhein.mmh`, 4 m, probed 2026-10-10): the hill south of the Hübel is a N–S ridge, 100–167 m between x −900 and −650, flanked by a valley at x ≈ −550 (95–145 m) and a dip at x ≈ −950 (80–110 m). The ground grid `TGRID` ends at **z = 2042** (`L.sdf`); beyond it nothing is drawn and `meshH` falls back to the clamped heightmap, so every room must stay north of z ≈ 2035. On the hill: no buildings, roads, rail, streams, props or water within the planned footprint (checked against `world_hochrhein.json`).
- **Importmap** maps `three/addons/` to `three@0.170.0/examples/jsm/` (`:320`): `loaders/GLTFLoader.js` and `libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js` are importable without any new dependency. The repo has no `prototype/assets/` yet; README lists geodata licences, "hand-made assets: licence to be decided".
- **The model** (maintainer, issue comment): „Eiffel Tower" (https://skfb.ly/AIU9) by Johnson Martin, CC BY 4.0, converted for desktop to `~/LocalSend/eiffel_tower.desktop.glb` (3.1 MB, 453 k triangles, metal/rough materials, `EXT_meshopt_compression` + `KHR_mesh_quantization`); verified to load with `GLTFLoader` + `MeshoptDecoder`, 330 m tall after scaling, base 139 × 137 m, z-up handled by the loader. Its materials are to be overridden with Eiffel brown (`#6b4a32`, roughness .75, metalness .35).

## Design

### Layout (top-down; +x east → right, +z south → down; game metres)

```
                 Hübel (cul-de-sac)  ───────────────────────────────────────  z ≈ 1419
                     │ mouth (−703.7, 1423.6)
                     │ T1  105 m · 9 m wide · 5 % down · open trench 0–12 m, gate, lid from the gate on  (unchanged, #102)
                     ▼
  N1 ◄──── T2a 90 m ──── R0  (−712.8, 1528.2)  r 22  Vorhalle: lamps, lights  ── stub E 15 m ─┤KEIN ZUTRITT   ··► [R2 Atomium  r 75 @ (−600, 1640)]
 (−802.5, 1520.4)         (the #102 cavern, tower removed)
  r 9
  ├── stub W 15 m ─┤KEIN ZUTRITT   ··► [R3 Titanic dock  300 × 80 m, N–S @ x −1150, z 1480–1780]
  │                                     [R4 free  r 45 @ (−690, 1700)]            (R2–R4: reserved, not built)
  │ T2b 170 m · heading ≈ 88° → the reveal: the lit tower fills the end of the tunnel
  ▼ door (≈ −817, 1687)
 ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
 │ R1 Eiffel hall   centre (−790, 1855)   r 170    │  floor 70.6 (= R0)  ·  shell ceiling floor + 380  ·  hill above it 119–167 m
 │          ▲ tower 330 m, base ≈ 139 m, 4 feet    │  4 floodlights + 2 base lights, dark fog, gravel floor
 └─────────────────────────────────────────────────┘  south edge z ≈ 2025 (ground grid ends at 2042)
```

Path from the gate to the tower: 105 + 90 + 170 + 170 ≈ 535 m, ~35 s at 16 m/s. Every floor is at **one level, `f0 = 70.6 m`** (the R0 floor of #102: ground at the mouth minus 5 % × 83 m), so overlapping cuts never step.

**Why the tower is not visible from the entrance:** T1 (heading 95°) ends in R0; the only way on is T2a, which leaves R0 at **185°** (a right-angle turn), and the hall lies beyond the bend N1. From the mouth the view cone through T1 (±7°) ends on R0's solid ring; R0's E stub is partly in view but 15 m long and walled. Pinned by a pure `sightBlocked()` test (every 2-D line from the mouth span to the hall disc crosses rock) and a browser ray test.

### Cave model (pure, `prototype/hideout.js`)

- `CAVE = { f0 from T1, nodes: [R0 {c, r 20 (+margin+wall/2 = 22), ceiling: 'lid'}, N1 {c: R0 + 90 m @ 185°, r 7 (→ 9), ceiling: 'lid'}, HALL {c: [-790, 1855], r 168 (→ 170), ceiling: 380, shellR: 167}], links: [T2a: R0→N1, T2b: N1→HALL, hw 4.5, ceiling 12], stubs: [E from R0 @ 45°, W from N1 @ 185°, len 15, hw 4.5, ceiling 'lid'] }`. T1 stays `HIDEOUT` (mouth, heading, length 105, hw 4.5, grade 0.05) and keeps `portalS`.
- `caveCuts(groundAt)` → `{ cuts: [T1 (as `hideoutCuts().tunnel`), R0 disc, N1 disc, T2a, T2b, HALL disc, stub E, stub W], f0 }`. Discs are the #102 trick (a 0.1 m polyline, `hw`, `flat: 1`, `roundEnds`); links are polylines `[from.c, to.c]` with `t: 0`, `flat: 1e9` (level), `roundEnds`; all `u: TUNNEL`, `depth = groundAt(centre) − f0`, `x, z` = the cut's midpoint. `cutFloorMin` already takes the minimum over overlapping cuts.
- `roomAt(x, z)` → the node id whose disc (+pad) contains the point, else null; `corridorAt(x, z)` → the link/stub whose corridor (`d ≤ hw + margin + wall/2 + pad`, `0 ≤ s ≤ len`) contains it; `caveRoofed(x, z, portal, pad)` = any room or corridor, or T1 with `s ≥ portal` (replaces `roofed`). `inHideout` (no trees, no forest-edge walls) shrinks to **the open trench only**: `d ≤ 10 && s ∈ [−4, portal + 6]` on T1. `inCavern(x, z, pad)` stays for R0.
- `ringArcs(node, exits)` generalises `ringArc`: exits = the links/stubs leaving the node (their headings and corridor half-widths) → the list of `{a0, a1}` arcs of wall between them (R0: T1 at 275°, T2a at 185°, stub E at 45° → 3 arcs; N1: T2a, T2b, stub W → 3 arcs; HALL: T2b → 1 arc). `ringStations` per arc as today.
- `linkWalls(link)` → `wallStations(link.pts, [[0, len]], hw + margin + wall/2, 2)` minus stations inside either end disc (`roomAt(…, −wall)`), the #102 corridor rule.
- `ceilingAt(x, z, meshH)`: in a node with `ceiling: 'lid'` → `meshH − 0.6`; in a link/stub with a numeric ceiling → `min(meshH − 0.6, f0 + 12)`; in the hall → `f0 + 380`; on T1 → `min(meshH − 0.6, floorAt(s) + 12)` from the portal on (the #102 trench keeps `meshH − 0.6` where that is lower). **The tunnel ceiling cap of 12 m is new** (today T1 is a 5–45 m slot whose ceiling is the hill surface) — `[needs maintainer]`, A4.
- `sightBlocked(a, b)`: walks the segment in 0.5 m steps; blocked if any sample is in rock, i.e. in no room disc and no corridor (T1's corridor counts from `s ≥ 0`).
- `EIFFEL = { h: 330, feet: [{ x: ±50, z: ±50, hw: 13, hd: 13 }] (relative, metres), footH: 60, url: './assets/models/eiffel_tower.glb', colour: 0x6b4a32, roughness: 0.75, metalness: 0.35, loadWithin: 400 }`. The foot boxes are **measured from the loaded model** in Task 5 (per-quadrant bounding boxes of vertices below 20 m) and the constants updated to the measurement; a browser test pins them (±2 m).
- `HALL_CAM = { tilt: 0.45, ease: 1 }` (see Camera). `TOWER_SPOTS`: four floodlight positions at radius 140 m on the diagonals, aimed at `(c, f0 + 100, c)`.

### Floor, lid, ceiling, walls (`index.html`)

- `HIDE` becomes `{ ...caveCuts(meshH), portal, found, counts…, cuts }`; `patchCuts()` returns `ARMS` plus every cave cut with `depth > 0`; `buildCuts` sets each cut's `bounds` exactly as for the two today. Nothing in `CUTS` (`__mm.crossings`, #119 walls, `test_underpass.py` untouched).
- **Lid** for every cave cut's patch cells: the uncut hill into `grass` (as today). **Ceiling copy** (reversed, into `stone`) for everything **except the hall**, with vertex `y = ceilingAt(x, z)` instead of `meshH − 0.6`, so tunnels get a flat 12 m ceiling and R0/N1/stubs keep the hill's underside. The hall has no ceiling copy: its shell (below) is the ceiling.
- **Triangle budget:** a patch cell whose four corners and centre are all on the flat cave floor is drawn as a `PlaneGeometry(dx, dz, 1, 1)` (2 triangles) instead of 16 × 16; likewise a lid cell wholly roofed (`meshH` is planar per grid triangle with the same split, so 1 × 1 is exact). Boundary cells keep 16 × 16. Target: the whole system adds **< 120 k triangles** to `grass` + `stone` (hooks `lidTris`, `floorTris`); without the shortcut the hall alone would add ≈ 460 k.
- **Walls:** `wallBox` (today's helper: stone box from the floor to `topAt`, `low` secret OBB) along every link/stub (both sides, skipping stations inside a disc), around every arc of R0 and N1 (`topAt = ceilingAt`), and one **end wall** across each stub at 15 m (full corridor width, floor to ceiling, solid OBB) carrying a yellow/black sign plane `textTex('KEIN ZUTRITT', 1024, 160, '#f2c230', '#1a1a1a', …)` (place-style, untranslated like the gate sign). The hall gets **no stone ring**: a ring of 48 invisible solid OBBs at radius 166 (`hd 1`, `h = f0 + 400`, not `low`, `secret`) keeps the car and the chase camera inside; the drawn wall is the shell.
- **Floor finish:** gravel ribbons on T2a/T2b/stubs (`ribbon`), the R0 disc as today, a gravel `disc(c, 167, 0.04, 'gravel', …, 48)` for the hall (`disc` gets a `segments` argument, default 14).
- **Static map:** the height loop (`:2099`) samples `HIDE && roofedAt(x, z) ? meshH(x, z) : terrainH(x, z)` — the hill, not the floor.
- **Trees:** scattered `TREES` still skip the whole system (`inHideout` → now also `roomAt || corridorAt`, rare on a wooded hill anyway). **Forest trees over the system stand on the lid**: their `t[3]` and the forest-edge wall heights use `lidGround(x, z) = roofedAt(x, z) ? meshH(x, z) : terrainH(x, z)`; only the open trench (`inHideout`) stays tree-free. So the hilltop over the hall is forest as before — no bald disc from the helicopter.

### The hall shell and cave mode (`CAVE` group, toggled)

- `CAVE.group` (a `THREE.Group` in the scene) holds everything of the hall that must not exist for an outside observer: the **shell** (two open cylinders `CylinderGeometry(167, 167, h, 64, 1, true, θ0, θlen)` — the lower 12 m as an arc with a gap at the T2b door, the rest to `f0 + 380` as a full circle — plus a `CircleGeometry(167, 64)` ceiling facing down, one `MeshLambertMaterial({ color: 0x3a3632, side: DoubleSide })` with per-vertex darkening jitter for a rock feel; **1 merged draw call**), the **tower** (placeholder or model), the **cave lights** (the hall's 4 `SpotLight`s + 2 `PointLight`s, and R0's two point lights moved in here), the lamp heads. Three's cylinder angle is `θ = π/2 − a` for a game heading `a`.
- `caveInside()` = `HIDE && roofedAt(P.x, P.z) && P.y < meshH(P.x, P.z) − 1` (the car is under the hill) — one function, evaluated each frame in `stepCar`; its change calls `setCave(on)`. `[#202 readiness]` a walker/hoverer rising above `meshH` inside the hall would read as "outside": #202 replaces the `y` test by a portal-crossing state in this one function; nothing else needs to change.
- `setCave(on)` (modelled on `setUnderwater`): `CAVE.group.visible = on`; fog `FogExp2(0x07080a, 0.0016)` (330 m: 75 % visible, 600 m: 40 %) or the style's; sky uniforms to `#07080a` or the style's; `sunSprite.visible = !on`; `hemi` → `(0x4a5468 sky, 0x1a1612 ground, 0.35)` or the style's; `sun.intensity = St.sunI × 0.15` (a faint "work light" from above so the top of the tower does not vanish) and `sun.castShadow = false` while inside (restored per style on exit, `renderer.shadowMap.enabled` too); every tree mesh (`treeBill/treeCone/treeTrunk`, each `FOREST_MESHES` entry) hidden while inside, re-shown per style on exit (`applyStyle` keeps the last cave state). `setUnderwater` and `setCave` are never both on (no water in the hill).
- **Shader pre-warm:** toggling light visibility changes every lit material's program; at build, with `CAVE.group.visible = true`, call `renderer.compile(scene, camera)` once, then hide it — both program sets are cached, no hitch at the door.
- **Lights (≤ 12 in cave mode, 0 outside):** four `SpotLight(0xffd9a0, 10000, 600, 0.5, 0.5, 2)` on the floor at radius 140 m on the hall's diagonals, each with a target at `(cx, f0 + 100, cz)` → ≈ 0.5 at the tower's first floor, 2 on the nearest leg base, 0.1 at the top (fades into the dark — intended); two `PointLight(0xffe2b0, 400, 120, 2)` at `f0 + 6`, ±60 m along the T2b axis, for the floor around the legs; R0's two existing lights. No light casts shadows. `__mm.hideoutLights()` reports all of them with `visible`.
- **Fog colour vs. shell:** both near-black, so the far wall and the ceiling fade out — the hall reads as a cavern without a measured end.

### The tower

- **Placeholder first:** `eiffelParts(330)` built as today but into **one merged `Mesh`** (`MeshLambertMaterial 0x6b4a32`) inside `CAVE.group`, not into the `dome` role — so it can be removed. `__mm.counts.eiffel = 1` either way.
- **Lazy load:** in `stepCar`, once `distance(P, mouth) < EIFFEL.loadWithin` (400 m: the Hübel, a J landing, or the hill above) and not yet started: `const [{ GLTFLoader }, { MeshoptDecoder }] = await Promise.all([import('three/addons/loaders/GLTFLoader.js'), import('three/addons/libs/meshopt_decoder.module.js')]); loader.setMeshoptDecoder(MeshoptDecoder); await loader.loadAsync(EIFFEL.url)`. On success: traverse, set every mesh's material to one shared `MeshStandardMaterial({ color, roughness, metalness })`, `castShadow = receiveShadow = false`, compute normals if missing; `Box3.setFromObject` → `scale = 330 / height`, position so the base sits on `f0` centred on the hall centre; swap the placeholder out; `CAVE.eiffel = { loaded: true, tris, height, base }`. On failure (offline, blocked): keep the placeholder, `console.warn`, `CAVE.eiffel.error`. No shadows, no mesh collision.
- **Collision:** four OBBs from `EIFFEL.feet` (`secret`, `h = f0 + 60`) — drive between the legs under the arches. Same boxes for placeholder and model (the placeholder's legs are drawn to the model's foot positions by passing the measured corner half-width to `eiffelParts`).
- **Asset:** `prototype/assets/models/eiffel_tower.glb` (3.1 MB, committed) + `prototype/assets/models/eiffel_tower.LICENSE.txt` (title, author, URL, CC BY 4.0 URL, "materials replaced in-game, meshopt-compressed"). **Credits:** README „Third-party assets" section, the CHANGELOG line, and in-game a small `creditEiffel` line at the bottom of the **F1 help** (`en`/`de`): „3D model „Eiffel Tower" by Johnson Martin (Sketchfab), CC BY 4.0".

## Consequences

- The hilltop over the system keeps its forest; the lid is the hill. The open trench and gate are the only visible change outside (none vs. today).
- Inside, the whole world is still submitted to the GPU each frame (occluded by the shell); draw calls rise by the shell, the tower's meshes and the lights, triangles by ~450 k (model) — desktop only.
- Toggling cave mode swaps fog/sky/lights/shadows/trees each time the car crosses the gate; the underwater mode's restore path (`applyStyle`) gains a second state to respect.
- `terrainH` over the system is the floor (as today over R0); everything that samples ground height over the hill must ask `skyGroundH`/`lidGround` — the map and trees now do; the autopilot/navi graph is roads only and unaffected.
- The 3.1 MB GLB is fetched once per session when a player approaches the Hübel (browser-cached afterwards); offline or blocked, the primitive placeholder stays.
- `localStorage['mm.hideout']` keeps its meaning (found = reached the hall); players who found the #102 cavern keep their flag although they never saw the big tower.
- #202 (walk/hover) inherits: a flat 340 m floor at 70.6, a 380 m air column, `caveInside()` as the single place to make stateful, `ceilingAt` for its own clamp, no mesh collision on the tower (walking onto the first floor needs its own platforms).
