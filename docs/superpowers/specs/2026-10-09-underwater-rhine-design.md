# Underwater world in the Rhine — design

Status: approved in `/enrich --quick` (no human gate) 2026-10-09 · Issue #101

## Goal

„Unterwasserwelt im Rhein." — a playtest wish (the 10-year-old's list, see #108): the Rhine should have something *under* its surface, and the player should be able to see it.

Success: you drive off the bank into the Rhine. The car splashes, „Sleep with the fishes!" shows as today, and then — instead of being put back on the road after three seconds — the car **sinks onto the riverbed and stays there**. The picture turns blue-green and murky, the surface shimmers overhead, and you creep along the bed at walking pace between boulders and waterweed, past schools of fish, and if you look for it you find a sunken rowing boat and a rusty old car lying nose-down in the deep. Where the bed rises to the bank, you simply drive back out onto the gravel; **R** puts you back on the last road, as always. Nothing new to learn: no new key, no new vehicle, no air meter.

## Starting point (verified 2026-10-09 on `main` @ `56a2af3`)

- **Water today ends the drive.** `stepCar` (`prototype/index.html:1355-1357`): with `waterLevelAt` non-null, `P.splash` counts up, `P.vx/vz *= 0.9` every physics step (the car stops within a second), `P.y` sinks to `wl - 1.2`, the `fishes` toast shows at 0.35 s and `resetCar()` fires at 2.8 s. `resetCar` (`:1247`) puts the car on `P.safe`, the last road position.
- **The bed is a number, not a thing.** `groundH` (`:553-561`) returns `max(wl - 3, fill)` inside the river (`:560`); the drawn terrain under the water is *not* lowered: on the OSM path `baseH` is the swissALTI3D height, which over the Rhine is the water surface itself (`:498`); on the hand path `baseH` is flattened to 0 towards the water (`:505`). The water surface is drawn at `level + 0.06` (`waterPolys`, `:595-608`; hand ribbon `:878`). So the car today sinks *through* the drawn grass and lies 1.2 m under a surface that, seen from below, does not exist.
- **The water surface is single-sided.** Both styles build the `water` role with the default `FrontSide` (`STYLES.original.mat`, `:1189`; `smooth`, `:1191`): from underneath it is culled, and so is the grass (`:1074`, Lambert/Standard default side). A camera under the surface today sees the sky through everything.
- **Camera.** The chase cam target is clamped *up* to `groundH + 1.2` (`stepCamera`, `:1382`) and never pulled under the surface; eye views sit at `P.y + ey` (`:1380`). Camera near plane 0.5, far 4200 (`:1066`).
- **Fog, sky, light are per style.** `STYLES[k].fog()` (linear 320–1400 m / `FogExp2 0.00095`), `sky` colours as uniforms of a back-side sphere that follows the camera (`skyMat`, `:1070`; `sky.position.copy(camera.position)`, `:1383`), `hemi` 0.9 / `sun` 0.8 (`:1067-1068`), all set in `applyStyle` (`:1194`), toggled with **T** (`:1207`). Village sprites are unfogged (`fog: false`, `:1060`).
- **Terrain mesh.** One `PlaneGeometry` at 16 m cells, vertex `y = terrainH`, vertex colour `groundCol(h)` by height (`:849-852`), merged into `MESH.grass`. `terrainH` reads `CUT_CELLS` patches (rail underpasses, `:485-493`, `buildCuts` `:531-540`) and otherwise `meshH` → the lazily built `TGRID.h` height grid from `baseH` (`:475-480`). Vertex colours are used in the original style only (`vertexColors` is false for `grass` in `smooth`, `:1191`).
- **Where the water is.** `riverDist(x, z)` (`:394`) — the pipeline's SDF on the OSM path (clamped at 120 m), the hand polyline `RIVER` otherwise — negative inside the water. `waterLevelAt(x, z, y)` (`:543`) is null on a bridge, the polygon level inside the Rhine (`WATER.levelAt`, `world.js:36-42`) or 0 on the hand path, and the draped stream height on the Sissle. `RIVER.pts` (`:331`) exists on both paths.
- **Hand-path shore.** The hand river is drawn as three ribbons: gravel `hw + 14` at 0.03, water `hw` at 0.06, current `hw - 6` at 0.08 (`:877-879`). The gravel ribbon is a flat sheet under the whole river width.
- **Helicopter (#10).** `takeOff` (`:1269`) works from anywhere (`ground = groundH`), `FLY.on` skips `stepCar` (`:1528`), the heli cam looks down from 22 m (`flyCamera`, `:1375`).
- **Sound.** `SFX.splash()` (`:1226`) is a filtered noise burst; the engine runs on through `SFX.update` while driving (`:1528`).
- **Strings.** `fishes` en/de (`strings.js:71`, `:196`); `strings.test.mjs` enforces equal keys and Swiss spelling.
- **Tests that touch water:** `test_smoke.py::test_osm_rhine_splash_and_overpass` (`:69-90`, asserts `y <= water - 1.1` and `splash > 0`), `test_smoke.py::test_fishes_text_while_car_lies_in_water` (`:116-130`, waits `splash > 0.6`), `test_i18n.py:175-181`, `test_toast.py:67-71` (same wait) — none of them asserts the reset. `test_vehicles.py` has a golden `stepCar` trace on land that must not change. `__mm.car()` (`:1324`) reports `splash`, `ground`, `water`; `__mm.sim` (`:1323`) steps `stepCar` headless; `__mm.place(x, z)` drops the car.
- **Pure-module pattern:** `prototype/heli.js` + `prototype/tests/heli.test.mjs` (`node --test`), browser checks in `prototype/tests/test_heli.py` on the hand layout (`open_hand`: world and terrain blocked, deterministic).

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| How you get there | **By driving in.** No submarine, no dive key, no dive mode: the Rhine is part of the world, so a car that drives into it keeps existing in it. The splash, the `fishes` toast and the sound stay exactly as they are. The 2.8 s `resetCar()` goes away. |
| The riverbed | A real floor. `bedDepth(d)` in a new pure module `prototype/underwater.js`: for `d = riverDist` (negative inside) the bed lies `min(UW.maxDepth, -d * UW.slope)` under the surface, `UW.slope = 0.3`, `UW.maxDepth = 6` — 0 at the shoreline, 6 m from 20 m in. `groundH` returns `max(wl - bedDepth(riverDist), fill)` instead of `wl - 3`. The **drawn terrain** is lowered to the same height: every ground vertex inside the Rhine (`riverDist < 0` and `waterLevelAt(x, z, 1e4) !== null`) gets `y = min(y, wl - bedDepth)` and the bed colour `#8c7b5a` (sand/gravel). The hand path's flat gravel sheet (`:877`) becomes two 14 m shore strips (`offsetPolyline(RIVER.pts, ±(hw + 7))`, half width 7, draped on the terrain) so it no longer floats over the bed. |
| Driving underwater | Once `P.y` has sunk to the bed the normal ground physics runs with water modifiers: top speed `× UW.topFactor` (0.2 → ~12 km/h for the compact), drag `UW.drag = 1.2 /s` instead of 0.12, no nitro boost and no flames. While sinking (`air`), the car sinks at `UW.sink = 3 m/s` and horizontal speed decays with the same drag (replaces `*= 0.9` per step). Steering is the normal ground steering (it already scales with speed). Out: drive up the bed to the bank — depth is 0 at the shoreline, so there is no step; or **R**. `P.safe` is only written on roads (`:1361`), as today. |
| Hint | 3 s after the splash, once per dive: toast `underwaterHint` — en „Drive up to the bank, or press R for the road." / de „Fahr ans Ufer hoch, oder drück R für die Strasse." (`TOAST_S.event`). |
| Underwater look | `UW.on` = the camera is under the surface (`camera.position.y < waterLevelAt(cam.x, cam.z, cam.y)`), evaluated every frame at the end of `stepCamera`; on change `setUnderwater(on)`: `scene.fog = FogExp2(UW.fogColor '#17505f', UW.fogDensity 0.035)` (~40 m visibility), both sky uniforms to the fog colour and `#2b7a86`, `hemi.intensity × 0.6`, `sun.intensity × 0.5`; off restores the style's values. `applyStyle` re-applies `setUnderwater(UW.on)` at its end so **T** underwater stays underwater. The `water` material becomes `side: THREE.DoubleSide` in both styles so the surface is visible from below as a bright, moving ceiling (the texture scroll `:1528` already runs). |
| Camera underwater | Chase / near: when the car is submerged (`P.y < wl - 0.5`) the target is pulled under the surface, `target.y = min(target.y, wl - UW.camUnder 0.8)` (after the existing ground clamp). Eye views need nothing (they sit at the car). The heli cam needs nothing (it is 22 m up; `UW.on` turns off by itself as the camera rises through the surface). |
| Bubbles | A `bubbles` group of 24 small white sprites (radius 0.15–0.35 m, `opacity 0.6`, `depthWrite false`) rising at 1.2 m/s from the car's rear while `P.splash > 0`; each is re-spawned at the car when it passes `wl`. Hidden on land and in flight. |
| Content — static | Built once after the world in `buildUnderwater()`, from a jittered 12 m sample grid over the terrain bounds, keeping points with `riverDist < -8`, `waterLevelAt !== null` and `!onBridge` (pure `bedSamples(bounds, step, inside, rnd)`). **Boulders:** every 6th sample, `DodecahedronGeometry(rr(0.6, 1.8))` squashed to 0.6 in y, half sunk, pushed into the existing `stone` role (merged, no per-frame cost). **Waterweed:** every 2nd sample, a tuft of 3–5 blades — `PlaneGeometry(0.25, rr(1.5, 3.5))` standing up, random yaw — in a new role `weed` (`MeshLambertMaterial` / `MeshStandardMaterial`, `DoubleSide`, colour `#2f6b3a`, `vertexColors`), one merged mesh. |
| Content — fish | `UW.schools = 14` schools placed along the sample set (every `floor(n / 14)`-th sample), each with `rr(8, 14)` fish, radius `rr(5, 12)`, speed `rr(0.3, 0.6)` rad/s, a colour from `['#b9c3cc', '#7d9a6a', '#d88a4a']` (silver, green, Rotfeder). One `InstancedMesh` for all fish (≤ 200 instances) of one merged fish geometry (body: `SphereGeometry(0.35, 8, 6)` scaled `(1.6, 0.6, 0.35)`, tail: a 0.4 m triangle), `instanceColor` per school. Pure `schoolPose(school, k, t)` returns `{ x, y, z, th }` — fish `k` on the school's circle at phase `k / n`, bobbing `0.3 sin`, heading tangent. Matrices are updated only while `UW.on` (nobody sees fish through a 0.96-opaque surface). |
| Content — wrecks | Two one-off props at the bed: a **rowing boat** on its side (half-cylinder hull `CylinderGeometry(0.9, 0.7, 5, 10, 1, false, 0, π)`, wood `#6b4a2a`, plus two thwarts) and a **rusty car** nose-down (two boxes `4.2×1.0×1.8` and `2.2×0.8×1.6`, rust `#6b3f2a`, no wheels, pitched 25°, 30 m downstream). Position: the midpoint of `RIVER.pts[9]`–`RIVER.pts[10]` (the bend below Bad Säckingen, in the water on both paths); if `waterLevelAt` is null there (a future region cut), the first bed sample instead. Both go into their own small `wreck` group (`MeshLambertMaterial`, a handful of meshes), not into the merged roles, so the test hook can report where they are. |
| Streams | Unchanged on purpose: on the Sissle `groundH` is the terrain (no river branch), so the car now splashes through at walking pace instead of sinking into the ground and resetting. |
| Race, heli, autopilot | The race clock runs on underwater (house rule: nothing is paused). `takeOff` works from the bed. Autopilot stays on roads. #105 (wreck at 100 %) is untouched: water adds no damage. |
| Sound | No new sound. The engine keeps running underwater (it is the kid's car, not a simulation). |
| Strings | One new key pair `underwaterHint` (en/de). `fishes` unchanged. |
| Test hooks | `__mm.underwater()` → `{ on, fogDensity, fish, schools, stones, weeds, wreck: [x, z], waterDoubleSide }`; `__mm.bed(x, z)` → `{ ground, water, depth }`. |

## Assumptions

Confidence = how likely the human is to disagree.

- **A1** [med] The player reaches the underwater world by **driving in**, and the car then stays on the bed until the player drives out or presses R — the 2.8 s auto-reset is removed. Rejected: a submarine vehicle (a `VEHICLES` row needs `checkVehicle`'s circle-collision procedural model and would show up in #7's picker; `index.html:1170`), a dive key (one more key for a thing the river should just *do*), a camera-only peek during the existing 2.8 s (nobody builds a world for a three-second cutscene). Evidence: `stepCar` `:1357` is the only place that ends the dive; `resetCar` `:1247` already handles R and J; `P.safe` is only written on roads `:1361`, so R always lands on a road.
- **A2** [med] Bed profile `min(6, 0.3 · |riverDist|)`: 0 at the shore, 6 m from 20 m in. Rejected: keeping the flat 3 m (no way out, no shelving bank, and a visible 3 m cliff at every shoreline vertex), a measured bed (none in the data; the swissALTI3D is the water surface, `:498`). 6 m is about the Rhine above the Säckingen weir; at `0.3` a 16 m terrain cell carries the bank without a step larger than the 1 m OSM kerb rule (`:1359`).
- **A3** [high] The drawn terrain is lowered to the bed, not covered by a second bed mesh. Evidence: the ground vertices are written in one loop (`:851`) that already sets `y` and a colour; a second mesh would z-fight with the grass that stays at surface height.
- **A4** [med] Underwater driving = ground physics with a 0.2 top-speed factor and 1.2 /s drag; the engine keeps running; no nitro. Rejected: buoyancy / floating (the car should lie on the bed, it is Midtown Madness, not a boat), a stall (the kid wants to drive around down there). Evidence: `stepCar` `:1350` computes `top` and the 0.12 drag in one line; the golden trace in `test_vehicles.py` runs on land and sees none of this.
- **A5** [high] The underwater look is switched by the **camera's** position, not the car's, and restored by the same function on the way up; `applyStyle` re-applies it. Evidence: fog, sky and lights are set in one place (`:1194`) and the sky sphere follows the camera (`:1383`); a car-based switch would show blue fog while the chase cam is still above the surface during the splash.
- **A6** [high] `water` becomes `DoubleSide` in both styles. Evidence: `:1189` and `:1191` build it with the default `FrontSide`; from below it is culled today.
- **A7** [med] Content is procedural from three.js primitives, static and merged (boulders into `stone`, weed into a new `weed` role), with fish as one `InstancedMesh` animated only while `UW.on`. Rejected: a vertex-shader sway for the weed (new shader for a detail nobody asked for), per-fish meshes (200 draw calls), glTF assets (none in the repo, no licensed assets). Evidence: `parts`/`push`/`MESH` merge pipeline `:580-581`, `:1074`.
- **A8** [med] Two wrecks (rowing boat, rusty car) at the bend below Bad Säckingen, built from boxes and a half-cylinder; not the compact's own model. Evidence: `buildCompact` (`:1120-1121`) writes blinker boxes into the global `BLINK` groups, so a second call would double the car's turn signals.
- **A9** [high] One new string pair through `tr()` with en and de, Swiss spelling; `fishes` stays. Evidence: `strings.test.mjs:8-22`.
- **A10** [high] No new key, no touch button, no sound, no HUD element. `#watername` already says „Rhein" (`:1522`).
- **A11** [med] The hint toast fires once per dive at `P.splash` 3 s. Rejected: a permanent on-screen hint (HUD clutter), none (the auto-reset is gone, so a kid who used to be rescued needs to be told once).

## Consequences

- The river is no longer a reset: a car can sit on the bed forever; the race clock runs on; the trip odometer counts the underwater metres.
- The Sissle becomes fordable at walking pace (the `fishes` toast still shows when you cross it, as it does today).
- `test_smoke.py`'s water tests stay green unchanged (they wait for `splash`, not the reset); `test_i18n.py` and `test_toast.py` likewise.
- In the smooth style the bed is grass-green, not sand: `grass` ignores vertex colours there (`:1191`). The fog makes it read as a dim floor anyway.
- The `water` mesh renders both faces everywhere, also from above: no visual change from above (front faces win the depth test), ~1 % more fill on the river.
- Village sprites (`fog: false`) stay visible underwater, floating in the blue — they are far away and read as „that way is Stein".
- `groundH` under the river changes from `wl - 3` to `wl - bedDepth`: the hand-path smoke test's `y <= water - 1.1` still holds at riverDist −92 (depth 6); any future test that pinned 3 m must use `__mm.bed`.
- Driving over the Holzbrücke or the Fridolinsbrücke is unaffected (`onBridge` comes first in `groundH` and `waterLevelAt`).
- Taking off in the helicopter from the bed climbs 120 m above the bed; the surface passes the camera and the look restores itself.
- On the OSM path (Rhine 100–150 m wide) roughly 700 boulders (~25 k triangles) and 2 000 weed tufts (~16 k); on the hand path (213 m wide) about twice that; ≤ 200 fish instances. Well under one extra frame-millisecond; the fish update costs nothing on land.

## Out of scope

A submarine or boat, an air meter / drowning, swaying weed, caustics or god rays, underwater sound, fish that react to the car, the Sissle as a diveable stream, a minimap change, a hub screenshot.
