# Helicopter extras — nitro, altitude, look-back, „fliegendes Auge" look, rotor sound — design

Status: approved in headless `/enrich` (quick mode, no human gate) 2026-10-03 · Issue #99

## Goal

The helicopter mode (#10, merged) gets the five additions from the playtest:

1. **N** (nitro) works in flight: the helicopter accelerates harder and flies faster while N is held, with blue exhaust flames like the car.
2. The HUD shows the **flight height**: metres above the ground and, when the measured terrain is loaded, metres above sea level.
3. The helicopter **looks like „Das fliegende Auge"** (the TV series' black gunship): a dark, low, sleek silhouette with a big bubble canopy, stub wings, a long tail boom with a swept fin and a tail rotor, a four-blade rotor and wheels — **without any weapons**, without any name, logo or livery. A low-poly model built in code, the way the car is.
4. A **subtle rotor sound** in flight, aligned with #14's sound design (engine muted, synthesized rotor thump).
5. **B** (look back, #65, merged) works in flight: the heli camera flips to the front and looks back past the helicopter, like the chase camera does for the car.

Success: in a run, **F** lifts off; the HUD's top-right plate reads „Height 120 m" (or „Height 120 m · 404 m a.s.l." in the real world); holding **W** + **N** accelerates the helicopter to 216 km/h with blue flames at the exhausts; holding **B** swings the camera round in front of the helicopter; the helicopter is a dark gunship-shaped machine with a glass bubble nose; the rotor can be heard, quietly, as soon as #14 is on `main`.

Hooks: `__mm.fly()` (`:1046`), `__mm.flySim(secs, hold)` (`:1047`), `__mm.cam()` → `{ view, d, back, look }` (`:1068`).

- **Neighbours:** #93 (drone camera, enriched): a fifth `CAM_VIEWS` entry and a `drone` path in the chase branch of `stepCamera`; its plan says „`flyCamera` and ‚C does nothing while flying' unchanged". #92 (mirror, enriched): `mirrorVisible({ flying: FLY.on, back: CAM.back, … })` hides the mirror in flight. #83 (pause, merged): `loop` returns early while `PAUSE.on` (`index.html:1242`), `openPause` clears all keys (`:1132`).

- **Brands (`docs/07-brands-and-permissions.md`):** the game shows no logos or protected names; the car's licence plate is the only text texture on a vehicle.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Nitro keys | `heliInput` gains `nitro` (**N** held, keyboard only like the rest of the flight). **N alone flies forward**, as nitro counts as gas for the car; **S** + **N** cancel to a hover (`axis`). |
| Nitro numbers | `HELI.topNitro = 60` m/s (216 km/h; plain 40 = 144 km/h) and `HELI.accelNitro = 24` m/s² (plain 12). Same ratios as the car in spirit (top ×1.5, acceleration ×2). Releasing N: the speed falls back to 40 at the plain `accel`, like the car's drag. No limit on nitro, like the car. |
| Nitro look | Two blue cone flames (`0x5ab8ff`, the car's flame material numbers) behind the two turbine exhausts, in a `heliFlames` group inside `heli`; `heliFlames.visible = nitro` every flight step; hidden on landing. |
| Nitro state | `FLY.nitro` (bool) and `P.nitro = input.nitro` in `stepFly`, so #14's `SFX.update({ …, nitro: P.nitro, fly: FLY.on })` can raise the blade rate. |
| Altitude where | A new line `#alt` in the top-right `#tr` plate under `#best`, shown only while `FLY.on` (`hidden` otherwise). Visible at every width (unlike `#gear`). No speedometer change. |
| Altitude what | `heliAltitude(y, ground, base)` in `heli.js` → `{ agl, asl }`: `agl = round(y − ground)` with `ground = groundH(P.x, P.z, 1e4)` (the same ground `__mm.fly()` reports), `asl = round(y + base)` when `base` is a finite number, else `null`. Text via `tr('altitude', agl, asl)`: „Height 120 m · 404 m a.s.l." / „Höhe 120 m · 404 m ü. M."; without a datum just „Height 120 m" / „Höhe 120 m". Updated every frame in `hud`. |
| Model | `buildHeli(g, rotor, tailRotor, flames)` next to `buildCompact`, built once at start into the existing `heli` group (no vehicle-table entry). Materials: body `MeshPhong 0x1c2230` (blue-black gunmetal, specular `0x4a5a78`, shininess 60), glass `MeshPhong 0x0e1824` (shininess 160, opacity 0.9), dark `MeshLambert 0x14171d`; two nav lights (`MeshBasic` red left, green right). **No `textTex`, no text, no decals** — the Playwright test asserts no mesh in `heli` has a `material.map`. |
| Model parts (model metres; x forward, y up, z right) | Fuselage: extruded side profile `[[3.9,1.0],[3.4,0.55],[2.0,0.4],[-1.6,0.45],[-2.2,0.9],[-2.0,2.2],[-0.8,2.5],[1.2,2.45],[2.6,1.9],[3.6,1.35]]`, depth 2.0 centred, bevel 0.08. Stub wings: boxes (1.2, 0.12, 1.6) at (0.2, 1.2, ±1.7), nothing hanging under them. Engine cowl: box (2.4, 0.6, 1.3) at (−0.4, 2.75, 0); two exhausts `Cylinder(0.18, 0.22, 0.5)` along −x at (−1.75, 2.75, ±0.45). Tail boom: `Cylinder(0.22, 0.38, 5.2, 8)` along x centred (−4.6, 1.9, 0), thin end at the tail. Fin: box (0.9, 1.6, 0.12) at (−6.8, 2.6, 0), `rotation.z = 0.35` (swept back). Stabiliser: box (0.5, 0.08, 2.0) at (−5.8, 2.0, 0). Tail rotor: group at (−6.9, 2.2, 0.25), two blade boxes (1.9, 0.04, 0.16) crossed, spinning about z. Main rotor: mast `Cylinder(0.14, 0.14, 0.7)` at y 3.1, hub `Cylinder(0.35, 0.35, 0.3)` at y 3.3, `rotor` group at y 3.45 with two blade boxes (12, 0.05, 0.3) at 0 and π/2 (four tips, radius 6). Wheels: `Cylinder(0.25, 0.25, 0.18)` about z at (2.6, 0.25, 0), (−0.6, 0.25, ±1.1), each on a strut box (0.08, 0.5, 0.08). Nitro flames: `Cone(0.16, 1.1, 10)` pointing −x at (−2.3, 2.75, ±0.45). |

## Assumptions

- **A1** [med] Nitro numbers `topNitro: 60`, `accelNitro: 24`; N alone flies forward. Rejected: the car's exact factors (×1.5 top, ×2.1 accel → 60 / 25 — rounded), a nitro climb boost (Space already climbs at 15 m/s; the issue says nitro, which is speed). Evidence: `index.html:912` (`top 60 / topNitro 90, accel 16 / accelNitro 34`), `:1074` (nitro counts as gas).
- **A2** [med] Altitude as a text line in the top-right `#tr` plate, shown only in flight, with both heights when the real terrain is loaded. Rejected: a plate in `#gear` (hidden under 900 px, `index.html:54`), an altimeter dial (a new instrument for one mode), AGL only (the datum `base` is in the terrain header already, `docs/08-pipeline-terrain.md:51`). Evidence: `index.html:113-117`, `:396`.
- **A3** [med] The model follows the series' silhouette (dark, low, bubble canopy, stub wings, four blades, wheels, swept fin) without weapons, text or livery; the sensor ball on the nose stands in for „the eye". Rejected: keeping the yellow helicopter and only recolouring it (the issue asks for the look), a glTF asset (`gltf: null` is reserved and unloaded, `index.html:907`), gun pods or a chin cannon (explicitly excluded). The screenshot gate exists because this is taste.
- **A4** [high] The model stays inside `rotorMargin` 8 (nose 3.9, tail rotor 7.85, blades 6). Evidence: `heli.js:3-4`, `heli.test.mjs:17-19`; a larger model would need a larger margin and would change `heliFloor` for every roof.
- **A5** [high] B in flight flips the heli camera the way the chase camera flips, with the same snap-on-change. Evidence: `stepCamera` already computes `back`, `snap`, `s` before the `FLY.on` branch (`index.html:1104-1106`); `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-03-look-back-design.md` Decisions „Chase, near", „Switching".
- **A6** [med] The rotor sound belongs to #14 and lands with it; #99 only sets „subtle" (`ROTOR.gain` 0.5) and the nitro rate bump, conditionally. Rejected: a rotor stand-in in today's `SFX` (it would be deleted by #14's rewrite of the whole `SFX` block weeks later and conflict with it until then), blocking #99 on #14 (four of five points do not need sound). Evidence: `2026-10-03-engine-horn-sound-design.md` A5 and the plan's Task 4; no `prototype/sound.js` on `main`.
- **A7** [med] „Dezent" = `ROTOR.gain` 0.5 (from 1.2) with the 5.5 Hz pulse, unchanged cutoff 260 Hz. A listening test in `test-todo.md` is the real gate; it is one number.
- **A8** [high] No new key, no touch button: flight stays keyboard-only (#10 spec A3); N and B are existing keys with the same meaning as on the ground.
- **A9** [high] New UI text goes through `tr()` with en and de entries (`strings.test.mjs` enforces equal keys, Swiss spelling). `keyHeli` is extended rather than a new help line added.
- **A10** [high] #83 pause, #92 mirror: no code in this issue; #93 drone: independent edits on one line of `stepCamera`.

## Consequences

- At 216 km/h the speedometer needle reaches 90 % of the dial; the 1.4 km fog horizon is crossed in about 23 s, so the overview blurs faster under nitro.
- `heliFloor` is untouched, but the new silhouette is longer and darker: from the chase camera it fills about the same picture as the yellow one (nose to tail 11.75 m vs 9.3 m).
- The car's and the helicopter's flames are two groups: `stepFly` hides the car's and shows its own, `resetCar`/landing hides the heli's via `heli.visible = false` (the group is a child).
- `P.nitro` is now written in flight too; the only reader on `main` is `stepCar`'s own line (it overwrites it) — #14's loop input becomes the second reader.
- `heli.test.mjs`'s `HELI` deep-equality and `heliInput` shape change by design (two fields added); every other node test and the whole `test_heli.py` stay as they are.
- #14's spec says `ROTOR.gain 1.2`; after this issue the agreed number is 0.5 — whichever of #14 / #99 lands second applies it.
- The screenshot is a committed PNG (~100–300 KB) under `docs/ai-notes/screenshots/`, a new folder beside `docs/ai-notes/feedback/assets/`.
- #93's plan statement „`flyCamera` unchanged" is stale once #99 merges; #93 does not edit `flyCamera`, so nothing breaks.

## Out of scope

A top-down or cockpit view in flight, touch controls for the helicopter, a heli row in the vehicle table or the car picker (#7), Doppler or 3D panning for the rotor, the engine sound itself (#14), an altimeter instrument, a nitro limit or bar.
