# Secret entrance in the Hübel with a hidden Eiffel Tower — design

Status: approved in `/enrich --quick` (no human gate) 2026-10-09 · Issue #102

## Goal

„In einem Hügel/Berg soll es einen Geheimeingang geben. Dort befindet sich z.B. der Eiffelturm als Modell." — a playtest wish (the 10-year-old's list, see #108).

Success: at the west end of the **Hübel** (a quiet residential cul-de-sac at the foot of the wooded hill south of Münchwilen) a plain stone gate labelled „RESERVOIR HÜBEL" sits in a short cutting in the hillside. It is on no map, in no list and has no sign pointing at it. Drive in, and the trench becomes a 9 m-wide tunnel that descends gently 100 m into the hill and opens into a cavern 44 m across and 45–65 m high — with a 33 m **Eiffel Tower** standing in the middle, floodlit, with room to drive between its legs. The first time the car rolls into the cavern a toast says you found the hideout, the find is remembered, and from then on **J** → `eiffel` lists „Eiffelturm" (Münchwilen) and puts the car on the Hübel facing the gate.

## Starting point (verified 2026-10-09 on `main` @ `42b68df`)

- **Terrain is a 16 m grid mesh; cuts replace cells by 1 m patches.** `terrainH` (`prototype/index.html:486-495`) returns the `CUT_CELLS` patch surface where one exists, else `meshH` (`:475-481`, the lazily built grid of `baseH` = the swisstopo `.mmh` via `realH`, `:471`). `buildCuts` (`:531-540`) makes one cut per rail/road crossing and fills every patch cell inside `cutBounds` with `min(meshH, cutFloorMin)`; `cutFloorMin` (`:529`) takes the lowest `cutFloorAt` over `CUTS` within a 200 m box of each cut's `x, z`. The cut model lives in `prototype/world.js:102-160`: a cut is `{ pts, t, hw, flat, f0, reach }` with `cutFloor(c, s, u) = f0 + u.grade·max(0, |s| − flat)` (`:122`), `cutFloorAt` null beyond `hw + margin + wall/2` of the polyline or outside `reach` (`:128-132`), and every function takes the parameter set `u` (default `UNDERPASS`, `:95`) — so a cut with its own grade and depth cap needs no new maths. **`cutFloorMin` calls `cutFloorAt(c, x, z)` without `u`** (`:529`): a hideout cut must carry its own `u` and `cutFloorMin` must pass it.
- **The ground mesh drops the coarse cells under cuts and draws the patches** (`:854-869`): the 16 m `PlaneGeometry` keeps only triangles whose centroid cell is not in `CUT_CELLS`; each patch cell is a `PlaneGeometry(dx, dz, 16, 16)` with `y = terrainH`, vertex colour `groundCol(h)` (`:850`), merged into `parts.grass`. Materials are per role and `FrontSide` (`STYLES`, `:1187-1192`): a surface seen from below is culled.
- **`groundH` needs no change for a floor.** `groundH` (`:555-563`) returns `max(terrainH, roadSurfH, fill)` on land — a lowered patch *is* the drive surface, which is exactly how the rail underpasses work. Nothing in the game knows a „roof": the chase camera is clamped *up* to `groundH + 1.2` (`stepCamera`, `:1381`) and never down; the helicopter's floor is `groundH + boxes` (`stepFly`, `:1271`; `heliFloor`, `heli.js:19-27`) and `takeOff` works anywhere (`:1269`).
- **Trough walls (#119)** are built per *road* from `CUTS` (`:960-979`): `wallStations` (`world.js:148-160`) along the road, a stone `box` from the floor to `ground + 1` (or the deck underside), and a `low` OBB each, skipped where `ground − floor ≤ 0.1`. The loop reads `c.road` and `c.deck`, and so does the debug hook `__mm.crossings` (`:1307-1312`) that `test_underpass.py` consumes — **hideout cuts must not be pushed into `CUTS`**.
- **Collision** is the OBB grid: `collide` (`:1344`) pushes the car out of boxes, rings and circles; `low` boxes are passed over above their top. `pushOBB` (`:628`) also feeds the static map, which paints every non-bridge OBB grey (`:1464`, `for (const o of OBB) { if (o.bridge) continue; … }`) — a ring of wall boxes in the hill would show up on the **Tab** map as a grey ring.
- **Trees** are random: forest wherever `terrainH > 18` (`:1029`), kept off roads, rail, streams and OBBs via `free()` (`:636`), then one trunk collider each (`:1032`). The hill at the Hübel is 75–135 m high → forest. A tree loop that reads `terrainH` after the cut would plant trees on the cavern floor.
- **Landmarks** are procedural primitives placed by `terrainH`: `dsmChimney`, `waterTower`, `siloTower`, `plattformTower` (`:700-735`) use `box`/cylinders/`bar()` and `addOBB`, built in the `if (L)` block at `:1014-1024`. Positions come from the pipeline's resolved anchors (`L.anchors.landmarks`, `pipeline/anchors.json`) — adding an anchor means rebuilding `data/world_hochrhein.json`, which needs the cached `.osm.pbf` and the pipeline; `VILLAGES` (`world.js:224-233`) shows the other pattern: hand-measured game coordinates as constants in a pure module.
- **The J list** is `landmarkEntries(LANDMARK_INFO, …)` (`landmarks.js:52-61`), computed once into the `const JUMP_ENTRIES` (`index.html:1238`); a source is an `anchor` key or a `building` id (`sourcePos`, `:43-50`); `jump: [x, z]` is a separate landing spot (Fridolinsbrücke, `:11`) and `jumpTo` (`:1244`) puts the car on the nearest jumpable road, turned to face the landmark (`faceToward`).
- **Persistence**: `localStorage` keys `mm.odo` (`:1236`), `mm.best2` (`:1386`), always in `try/catch`.
- **Strings**: `strings.js` `en`/`de` objects, `fishes` at `:71`/`:196`; `strings.test.mjs` enforces equal keys and Swiss spelling. Toasts: `toast(html, secs)`, `TOAST_S.event = 5` (`:1389`).
- **Signs**: `textTex(txt, w, h, bg, fg, font, border)` (`:328`); `busStop` (`:837`) and `hallenbad` (`:698`) put text on planes.
- **Test hooks**: `__mm.sim(x, z, th, v, secs)` (`:1323`) drives the car and returns its state; `__mm.probe(x, z)` (`:1305`) returns `terrain`; `__mm.wallRoleAt` (`:1303`) ray-tests a role; `__mm.cam()` (`:1341`); `__mm.jumpList()` (`:1444`, after **J** opened the dialog); `__mm.counts.<name>` is how a landmark counts itself (`siloTower`, `:713`). Browser tests that need the real world and terrain gate on both files (`test_underpass.py:19`).
- **Where the hill is** (probe of `data/terrain_hochrhein.mmh` + `world_hochrhein.json`, 2026-10-09): the Hübel is OSM `residential`, `w 5.5`, 22 points from Münchwilen (`[-395.1, 1455.3]`) west to a dead end at `[-763.9, 1418.1]` by a small car park (ring around `[-770, 1420]`). At `[-703.3, 1419.1]` on its centre line the ground is 74.8 m (game height, +284 = 358.8 m a.s.l.); the hill rises due south (heading 95°) +7.4 m at 20 m, +19 at 40 m, +28 at 60 m, +48 at 100 m, +58 at 120 m, evenly across ±30 m. No building within 250 m, no junction within 80 m, no road other than the Hübel within 45 m of the corridor, no stream, props or rail (nearest 214 m). The summit (165.9 m, 450 m a.s.l.) is 400 m further on. Alternatives scanned: the Schupfarterstrasse climb out of Münchwilen (steep, but a through road with houses 300 m off), the Rüttistrasse in Eiken (houses within 100 m), Ankengasse Mumpf and the Harpolinger Straße by Murg (too far from the race route).

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Which hill | **The wooded hill south of Münchwilen, entered from the west end of the Hübel.** Reasons: it is the steepest hillside next to a quiet road inside the race area (48 % over the first 120 m), the road is a cul-de-sac nobody drives for a reason, there is nothing on the hill to collide with (no buildings, roads, rail or streams in the cut), and the cavern ceiling comes out at 45–65 m with no help. OSM names no peak or wood here, so the game calls the place by the road: „Hübel". |
| Geometry (constants in `prototype/hideout.js`) | `HIDEOUT = { mouth: [-703.7, 1423.6], heading: 95° (1.6581 rad; the car's `th` convention, +x = 0), length: 105, hw: 4.5, grade: 0.05, cavernHw: 20, roofDepth: 5.5, towerH: 33, found key 'mm.hideout' }`. `TUNNEL = { ...UNDERPASS, grade: 0.05, maxDepth: 80 }` is the `u` for the hideout cuts (`margin 1`, `wall 2` as the underpasses). The mouth is the hill-side road edge (centre line + `w/2 + margin`); the axis runs 105 m at 95° to the cavern centre `[-712.8, 1528.2]`. |
| Floor | **Two cuts, same model as #76/#119, in their own array `HIDE_CUTS`.** *Tunnel*: `pts: [mouth, centre]`, `t: 105`, `hw: 4.5`, `flat: 0`, `f0 = meshH(mouth) − 0.05·105` → the floor equals the ground at the mouth and descends at 5 % to 70.6 m at the cavern (5 m below the road). *Cavern*: a 0.1 m polyline through the centre along the axis, `t: 0.05`, `hw: 20`, `flat: 1`, same `f0` → `cutFloorAt` is a flat disc of radius `20 + margin + wall/2 = 22`. Both get `u: TUNNEL`, `reach` from `cutReach` (tunnel `[105, 0]`), `depth = meshH(centre) − f0` (≈ 58), `x, z` = centre. `cutFloorMin` iterates `[...CUTS, ...HIDE_CUTS]` and passes `c.u ?? UNDERPASS`; `buildCuts` fills the patch cells of both bounds. Everything downstream (`terrainH`, `groundH`, the drawn patches, roads draped later) works unchanged. |
| Roof | A **lid** drawn over the roofed part only: for every patch cell of the two cut bounds, the same `PlaneGeometry(dx, dz, 16, 16)` at `y = meshH` (the *uncut* hill surface), keeping only triangles whose centroid is `roofed(x, z)`; vertex-coloured like the ground and merged into `parts.grass` — from outside the hill looks untouched. A second copy 0.6 m lower with the triangle winding reversed (vertices 1 and 2 swapped), coloured dark rock `#4a4642`, merged into `parts.stone` — from inside it is the cave ceiling and needs no new material. `roofed(x, z)` (pure): inside the cavern disc (`≤ 22 + wall` from the centre) or on the tunnel corridor (`d ≤ hw + margin + wall/2 = 7.5` from the axis) with `s ≥ portalS`; `portalS` = the first whole metre along the axis where `meshH − floor ≥ roofDepth` (≈ 12 m on the probe). Patch and lid only ever coincide at the corridor boundary vertices, so nothing z-fights. |
| Walls | Tunnel: `wallStations(axis, [[0, 105]], hw + margin + wall/2, 2)` → a stone `box` per station from the floor to `top`, where `top = ground + 1` in the open trench (`s < portalS`, a parapet) and `meshH − 0.6` under the lid, plus a `low` OBB each (as #119, `:970-977`, but without the deck branch); pieces with `ground − floor ≤ 0.1` skipped (the first metres at the mouth). Cavern: `ringStations(centre, 22 + wall/2, 24)` (pure) → 24 stone boxes of ≈ 6 m around the disc from the floor to `meshH − 0.6` with an OBB each. Every hideout OBB carries `secret: true`. |
| Portal | At `s = portalS` across the trench: two stone **piers** `1 × 1.2` m filling the margin on each side (`d = ±(hw + 0.5)`) from the floor to `floor + 5`, a stone **lintel** `2·(hw + margin + wall) = 15` m wide, `1.2` m thick, from `floor + 5` to `meshH(portal) + 1` — the 1 m above the hillside is the parapet a real reservoir gate has. The opening is the full 9 m floor, nothing solid in it. A sign plane `6 × 0.9` m on the lintel's outer face: `textTex('RESERVOIR HÜBEL', 1024, 160, '#d8d2c6', '#2a2a2a', '700 110px "Barlow Condensed", sans-serif')` — the disguise: it reads as a water-works entrance, which real Swiss hillsides have. |
| Floor finish | A `gravel` ribbon `ribbon(axis, hw, 0.04, 'gravel', …, 8, true)` down the tunnel and a `disc(centre, 21, 0.04, 'gravel', …)` on the cavern floor, both draped on `terrainH` as the Plattform's pad is (`:725`). Not registered as a road surface. |
| Light | Two `THREE.PointLight(0xffd9a0, 1.4, 70, 1.5)` at `(centre ± 12 m along the axis, floor + 3)` in the scene, and four lamp posts (a 2.6 m `CylinderGeometry(0.05)` pole + a `SphereGeometry(0.35)` `MeshBasicMaterial 0xffd27a` head, in `signs`) at the four diagonals 15 m out — the chimney's aviation light pattern (`:704`). No fog or sky change: the cave is enclosed geometry, the ceiling and walls are dark rock, the floor is gravel. |
| The Eiffel Tower | `eiffelParts(h)` (pure) returns primitive descriptors at 1:10 of the real proportions, base `y = 0`, centre `(0, 0)`, for `h = 33`: four **legs** of three tapered segments each (`{ kind: 'leg', from, to, r0, r1 }`, rendered as `CylinderGeometry(r1, r0, len, 4)` turned along `from→to` like `bar()` in `plattformTower`, `:724`): base corners `±6.25` → first floor corners `±3.5` at `y 5.7` (`r 0.9→0.6`) → second floor `±2.0` at `y 11.5` (`0.6→0.4`) → top `±0.9` at `y 27.6` (`0.4→0.25`); three **platforms** (`{ kind: 'box' }`): `7.6 × 0.6 × 7.6` at `5.7`, `4.4 × 0.5 × 4.4` at `11.5`, `2.2 × 0.4 × 2.2` at `27.6`; four **arches** (`{ kind: 'arch', side }`): `TorusGeometry(4.4, 0.22, 6, 16, π)` standing under the first floor on each face, crown at `y 4.6`; **X braces** (`kind: 'leg'`, `r 0.12`) on each face of the first and second segments, corner to diagonal corner; a **spire** `CylinderGeometry(0.08, 0.2, 5.4, 6)` from `27.6` to `33`. Colour Eiffel brown `#6e4b2a`, role `dome` (the plain-coloured Lambert the church domes use, `:642`) so it reads as painted iron, not stone. Collision: one `addOBB` per leg foot (`1.6 × 1.6`, `h = 5.7`) — you can drive between the legs under the arches. `window.__mm.counts.eiffel = 1`. |
| Finding it | **Nothing points at it.** No map marker (the static map skips `secret` OBBs), no J entry, no help line, no label. The CHANGELOG entry gives one hint („a quiet road at the foot of a hill south of the A3", „a plain stone gate"). The gate is visible from the road once you drive to the end of the Hübel. |
| Discovery | In `stepCar` after `collide(carRadius())` (`:1359`): if `!HIDEOUT_STATE.found` and the car is inside the cavern disc (`≤ 22` from the centre, pure `inCavern`), set `found`, store `localStorage['mm.hideout'] = '1'` (try/catch), `toast(tr('hideoutFound'), TOAST_S.event)`, `SFX.finish()` (the existing jingle), and rebuild the J entries. On load `found` is read from `localStorage` the way `mm.odo` is. |
| J entry | `LANDMARK_INFO` gets `{ name: 'Eiffelturm', gemeinde: 'Münchwilen', game: [-712.8, 1528.2], jump: [-703.3, 1419.1], secret: true }`. `sourcePos` accepts `game: [x, z]`; `landmarkEntries(info, anchors, buildings, opts = {})` drops `secret` items unless `opts.secrets` is true. `JUMP_ENTRIES` becomes `let` and is rebuilt on discovery. **J** → the nearest jumpable road to the `jump` spot is the Hübel, `faceToward` the cavern → the car faces the gate. The autopilot (**O**) lists the entry too once found; it routes to the Hübel. |
| Camera | In `stepCamera`'s chase/near branch, after the ground clamp: `if (roofed(target.x, target.z)) target.y = Math.min(target.y, lidH(target.x, target.z) − 1)` where `lidH = meshH − 0.6` (the ceiling). Eye views need nothing. The camera's OBB test already pulls it in front of the cavern walls. |
| Helicopter | `skyGroundH(x, z) = roofed(x, z) ? Math.max(groundH(x, z, 1e4), meshH(x, z)) : groundH(x, z, 1e4)` replaces `groundH` in `stepFly` and `takeOff`, so the heli flies over the hill, not into the cavern. **F** inside the hideout: `toast(tr('hideoutNoSky'))` and no take-off. Landing over the hill finds the Hübel (`landRadius 150`). |
| Trees | The tree loop skips `inHideout(tx, tz)` (pure): corridor `d ≤ 10` from the axis for `s ∈ [−4, 105]`, or `≤ 26` from the centre. |
| Map | The static map's OBB loop skips `o.secret` as it skips `o.bridge`. The minimap/Tab map shows the hill as today. |
| Scope guards | Built only when `L && REAL` (the OSM world with the measured terrain) — the hand layout and the made-up terrain have no such hill. `__mm.hideout()` is null otherwise. Hideout cuts live in `HIDE_CUTS`, never in `CUTS`: `__mm.crossings`, the #119 wall loop and `test_underpass.py` see no change. |
| Strings | `hideoutFound`: en `Hideout found!<br>${small('The Eiffel Tower. In a hill. Of course.')}` / de `Versteck gefunden!<br>${small('Der Eiffelturm. In einem Hügel. Natürlich.')}`; `hideoutNoSky`: en `No sky down here – drive out first` / de `Hier unten gibt es keinen Himmel – fahr zuerst raus`. |
| Test hooks | `__mm.hideout()` → `{ mouth, centre, portalS, floor, lid, found, walls, lamps }`; `__mm.roofed(x, z)`; `__mm.lidAt(x, z)` (ceiling height or null); `__mm.skyGround(x, z)`; `__mm.counts.eiffel`; existing `__mm.sim`, `__mm.probe`, `__mm.wallRoleAt`, `__mm.cam`, `__mm.jumpList`. |

## Assumptions

Confidence = how likely the human is to disagree.

- **A1** [med] The hill is the one south of Münchwilen, entered from the Hübel cul-de-sac. Rejected: the Schupfarterstrasse climb (a through road, houses 300 m away), Eiken's Rüttistrasse (houses within 100 m), Mumpf/Murg (outside the race area). Evidence: the terrain probe above (`+58 m over 120 m`, nothing in the corridor); `VILLAGES` (`world.js:231`) puts Münchwilen at `(-299, 1408)`, 400 m east.
- **A2** [med] Gemeinde „Münchwilen" for the J entry. The Hübel runs into Münchwilen and the OSM place node is 400 m east; the Stein/Münchwilen border was not resolved from `L.boundaries` (polylines, not rings). If the Gemeinde is wrong it is one word in `LANDMARK_INFO`.
- **A3** [high] The floor reuses the #76/#119 cut model (`world.js:102-160`) with its own parameter set, not a new terrain feature. Evidence: every cut function takes `u`; `cutFloorAt` is null beyond the polyline's reach, so a dead end costs nothing; `buildCuts` (`:531-540`) and the patch drawing (`:854-869`) are generic over `CUT_CELLS`.
- **A4** [high] Hideout cuts are kept out of `CUTS`. Evidence: `__mm.crossings` (`:1307`) reads `c.deck.r.pts` and `c.road.n` of every entry, and the wall loop (`:965`) calls `deckOver`; both would throw on a deckless cut.
- **A5** [med] The roof is a lid of the uncut hill surface plus a dark, reversed-winding copy as the ceiling, both merged into existing roles. Rejected: `DoubleSide` on `grass` (every ground triangle rendered twice for one hill), a separate cave mesh/material (new role in both `STYLES` maps), leaving the trench open (then it is a pit, not a secret). Evidence: `FrontSide` default (`:1189-1191`); `parts.grass`/`parts.stone` merge (`:581`, `:867`).
- **A6** [med] The tower is 1:10 (33 m) from boxes, 4-sided tapered cylinders, torus arches and a spire, brown, role `dome`; the car can drive between the legs. Rejected: a lattice from hundreds of bars (draw cost for a thing seen once), a larger scale (the ceiling is 45 m at the disc's edge), a glTF (no assets, CLAUDE.md). Evidence: `plattformTower`'s `bar()` (`:724`) and `dome` role (`:642`) exist.
- **A7** [med] The disguise is a „RESERVOIR HÜBEL" reservoir gate at the end of a 12 m cutting, nothing on any map or list until found. Rejected: a fake rock door you drive through (reads as a bug), a hint in the F1 help (not secret), a marker on the full map (not secret). The sign text is a place-style name and stays untranslated like `HALLENBAD SISSILA` (`:698`).
- **A8** [high] Discovery is persisted in `localStorage` (`mm.hideout`) and reveals a J/O entry; nothing else is gated. Evidence: the `mm.odo` / `mm.best2` pattern (`:1236`, `:1386`); `landmarkEntries` is the single source of the J and O lists (`:1238`, `autoGraph`).
- **A9** [high] Camera and helicopter get a ceiling clamp / sky ground; take-off inside is refused with a toast. Evidence: `stepCamera` only clamps upward (`:1381`); `stepFly` floors on `groundH` (`:1271`) which would be the cavern floor under the lid.
- **A10** [high] OSM path with measured terrain only. Evidence: `buildCuts` runs `if (L)` (`:541`); the made-up `baseH` (`:499-506`) has no hill there.
- **A11** [med] Two point lights and four lamp posts, no fog/sky change inside. Rejected: a per-frame „inside" look switch like #101's underwater fog (the cave is closed geometry, the dark ceiling and walls do the job).

## Consequences

- The `grass` role gains ~2 × 16 × 16 × 2 triangles per lid cell (≈ 40 cells): a few thousand triangles, static, one draw call as before.
- `cutFloorMin` now loops two more cuts for every patch vertex of every cut (rail cuts included) — a build-time cost of a few ms; the 200 m box test rejects them for all rail cuts.
- The Hübel's last 60 m get a 1 m stone parapet on the hill side where the trench opens; the road ribbon itself is draped after the cut and follows the (unchanged) ground.
- Trees no longer grow on a 10 m strip south of the Hübel and on the hilltop disc above the cavern — the lid is forest-coloured ground without trees.
- In the smooth style the lid top is flat green like all grass there; the cavern floor is gravel in both styles.
- The autopilot can be sent to „Eiffelturm" once found; it stops on the Hübel at the gate, as it stops at any off-road landmark.
- `localStorage` gains one key; clearing site data forgets the find (the J entry disappears again until the next drive in).
- The race clock, checkpoints and damage are untouched; the cavern walls hurt like any wall.
